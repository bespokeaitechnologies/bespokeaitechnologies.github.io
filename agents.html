Forward Deployed Engineering

Engineers who work inside your business

We evaluate your current footprint first, then build the AI and software that fits it, side by side with your team.

The engagement

Four phases, one accountable team

01

Evaluate your footprint

We catalogue your systems, data, integrations and processes, including e-commerce front ends, back-office tools and the spreadsheets nobody documented. Output: a prioritised opportunity map.

02

Design with your people

Workshops with the people who do the work. We write the requirements and architecture so both humans and agents can build from them.

03

Build & deploy

Embedded engineers ship in short cycles using our software factory, from first pilot to production, with quality gates at every step.

04

Operate & evolve

Hand it to your team, or let us run it under managed IT. Either way, we keep improving it as the business changes.
