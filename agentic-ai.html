What we mean by an agent

Software that reasons, plans and acts, safely

A chatbot answers. An agent finishes the job: it reads the invoice, checks the purchase order, queries the ERP, drafts the exception, and routes the approval. It does that across several systems, within permissions you control.

We build agents that are useful on day one and boring in production: logged, evaluated, cost-aware and easy for your team to supervise.

How we build agents

From outcome to production in five steps

01

Pin the outcome

One process, one measurable result, one owner. We write down what success and failure look like before any model is chosen.

02

Connect tools and data

We wire the agent to your systems through governed APIs, with least-privilege access and a clean data layer behind it.

03

Build and evaluate

We build against a test set drawn from your real cases, and measure quality, cost and latency on every change.

04

Add guardrails

Approval gates for risky actions, spend limits, fallbacks and a human review queue. Agents earn autonomy as the evidence builds.

05

Deploy and operate

We host it, monitor it, and keep improving it. Quality, cost and drift are reported to you, not discovered by you.
