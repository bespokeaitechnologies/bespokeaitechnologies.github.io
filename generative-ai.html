Our approach

The right model for the job, not the biggest one

Most business problems don’t need a frontier model. Some need retrieval over your documents. Some need a model tuned to your language and format. Some are better served by a small, fast model you run yourself.

We start with an honest baseline, build an evaluation set from your real cases, and let the evidence pick the approach. Then we put it in production and keep it healthy.

Which approach?

An honest guide to choosing

We recommend the simplest option that works. Often that’s the first one.

Start with RAG

If the model needs to know your documents or data, retrieval is usually faster, cheaper and easier to keep current than training.

Fine-tune when behavior matters

When you need a consistent style, format or specialist skill that prompting can’t deliver reliably, tuning earns its cost.

Go small for scale and privacy

For high-volume, narrow tasks, an SLM can match a large model at a fraction of the cost, and run entirely inside your boundary.

How a project runs

From data audit to production

01

Audit the data

What you have, where it lives, who can see it, and what’s clean enough to use. This often decides the approach.

02

Baseline and eval set

We build a test set from your real cases and measure a simple baseline, so every later gain is real.

03

Build the system

RAG pipeline, fine-tune, small model or agentic app, whichever the evidence supports.

04

Harden

Guardrails, red-teaming, permissions, cost limits and a human review path for risky outputs.

05

Deploy and operate

We host it, monitor quality and cost, and retrain or re-index as your data and needs change.
